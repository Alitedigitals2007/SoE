🏟️ STADIUM OF ELITE — COMPLETE MATCH SYSTEM
============================================

⚽ 1. Match Setup
----------------

Every match has:

*   **2 teams**
    
*   **8 players per team**
    
    *   5 active players
        
    *   3 substitutes
        
*   1 referee
    
*   20 prepared questions
    
*   **10 questions played**
    
*   Match score
    
*   Match timeline
    

The captain is one of the 8 players.

👥 2. Players Enter the Match
=============================

Before kick-off, the 5 starting players join the match.

The 3 substitutes remain on the bench.

A substitute **cannot answer questions while on the bench**.

Once a substitution happens and the player enters the active lineup:

> They can answer the next available questions.

They remain eligible until they are substituted out.

🟨 16. MATCH CONDUCT
====================

Keep this simple.

The referee has:

> **⚖️ Match Conduct**

Possible incidents:

*   Answering outside allowed time
    
*   Attempted answer manipulation
    
*   Answer sharing
    
*   Abusive behaviour
    
*   Chat/spam disruption
    
*   Account misuse
    
*   Unauthorized assistance
    
*   Disrespect toward referee
    
*   Other
    

Possible action:

*   Warning
    
*   Yellow Card
    
*   Red Card
    

A red-carded player leaves the match.

🔥 THE COMPLETE QUESTION CYCLE
==============================

This is now the core mechanic of Stadium of Elite:

**QUESTION OPENS**↓**COUNTDOWN STARTS**↓**ELIGIBLE PLAYERS ANSWER**↓**ANSWERS ARE LOCKED**↓**COUNTDOWN ENDS**↓**PLAYERS SEE ANSWERS WITHOUT NAMES**↓**REFEREE SEES ANSWERS + PLAYER NAMES + SUBMISSION TIMES**↓**EVERYONE WAITS**↓**REFEREE SELECTS THE VALID ANSWER OR NO GOAL**↓**DECISION IS REVEALED**↓**IF GOAL → PLAYER NAME + ANSWER + SCORE REVEALED**↓**IF NO GOAL → CORRECT ANSWER REVEALED**↓**TIMELINE + SCORE + STATS UPDATE**↓**NEXT QUESTION**

That gives Stadium of Elite its own identity: **the suspense isn't just answering the question; it's waiting to discover whose answer the referee accepts and whether it changes the score.**
